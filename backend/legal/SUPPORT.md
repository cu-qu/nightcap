# NightCap Support

Need help with the app, your account, or a subscription?

**Email:** [night_cap_app@proton.me](mailto:night_cap_app@proton.me)

Please include your NightCap username. We read this inbox for password resets, billing, and account questions.

## Subscriptions

NightCap is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google charges you, not NightCap directly.

- Restore a purchase you already paid for: open NightCap → **See plans** → **Restore purchases**
- Cancel or change plans: iPhone **Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions**, or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions
- Offer codes: on iPhone, NightCap → **Redeem Apple offer code**

## Delete your account

In the app: **Settings → Delete account**. That removes your journal, photos, and login. You can also email us and ask us to delete the account.
