# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 12, 2026

NightCap (“we”, “us”) is a private end-of-day journal for one person or a couple. This policy describes the information the NightCap apps and API collect, how we use it, and how you can delete it.

Contact: [night_cap_app@proton.me](mailto:night_cap_app@proton.me)

## Information we collect

**Account.** Username, email address, password (stored as a one-way hash), language preference, and whether you use NightCap alone or with a partner.

**Journal and tracking.** Categories you create, daily spend and habit entries, amounts, quantities, notes, moods, favorite moments, and goal targets. Workout and fitness numbers are values you type in. We do not connect to Apple Health or HealthKit.

**Photos.** If you add a favorite photo of the day, we store that image on our servers so it can sync and appear in recaps. We access the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo.

**Partner sharing.** If you join a couple space, we store the invite code and the other person’s username and email so you can link accounts. Shared goals and together check-ins include both people’s matching category values. Favorite photos stay private to the person who uploaded them.

**Notifications.** If you turn on the nightly reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. We do not send marketing push campaigns.

**Subscriptions.** If you subscribe, Apple or Google processes the payment. We receive a store transaction identifier, product id, expiry, and auto-renew status so we can unlock NightCap. We do not receive your card number.

**Technical.** Authentication tokens on the device (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), and standard server logs needed to run the API (IP address, timestamps, error traces). We do not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

## How we use information

We use this information to provide NightCap: sign-in, sync, couple goals, recaps, customer support, security, and subscription entitlement. We do not sell personal data. We do not use your journal, photos, or spend logs for advertising.

## Legal bases and sharing

We share data only with:

- **You and, if you choose, your linked partner** for together goals and check-in values.
- **Apple and Google** for in-app purchases (they are the merchant of record).
- **Infrastructure providers** that host the API, database, file storage, and transactional email, under contracts that limit use to providing the service.

We may disclose information if required by law.

## Retention

We keep account and journal data while the account is open. Photos remain until you remove them or delete the account. Server logs are kept only as long as needed to operate and secure the service.

## Your choices

In the app you can edit or delete individual nights, photos, goals, and partner links. You can delete your entire account in **Settings → Delete account**. That removes your account, journal, photos, and tokens. If you were linked with a partner, the link is removed; their own account stays unless they delete it too.

You can also email [night_cap_app@proton.me](mailto:night_cap_app@proton.me) to request access, correction, export, or deletion.

## Children

NightCap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes. The effective date at the top will change.
