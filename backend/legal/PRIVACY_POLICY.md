# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 10, 2026

NightCap stores account information and the tracking data you choose to sync, including
categories, daily entries, notes, dates, amounts, and custom values.

We use this information to provide syncing, account access, exports, and service
maintenance. Passwords are stored as secure hashes. We do not sell personal data.

You can request access, correction, export, or deletion of your account data by
contacting privacy@nightcap.app.
